FAQ

Questions, answered

Can’t find what you need? Write to us — a human replies within a day.

Can I try Lumen before paying?

Every paid plan starts with a 14-day trial that includes the full Team feature set. The Starter plan is free forever, so you can keep using Lumen for personal meetings after the trial ends.

What counts as a meeting?

Any calendar event with two or more attendees that Lumen is invited to. Recordings, voice memos, and one-on-ones are all supported. Starter caps each meeting at 60 minutes and 10 meetings per month.

How does per-seat billing work when people leave?

Seats are billed in advance. Remove someone mid-cycle and the seat is freed for a teammate right away; on annual plans the unused credit is applied at renewal. Admins can add or remove seats from the billing page without talking to us.

Can we switch between monthly and annual billing?

Yes, at any time. Moving to annual applies the 20% discount immediately and prorates the rest of the current month. Moving back to monthly starts with the next billing cycle.

Where is our data stored?

EU customers keep meeting data in Frankfurt; everyone else in us-east-1. Transcripts and recordings are encrypted at rest with AES-256 and in transit with TLS 1.3, and are never used to train models.
